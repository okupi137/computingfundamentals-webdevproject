Lineup


Day One:

Playboi Carti

Playboi Carti kicks off day 1 with atmospheric beats and an unforgettable performance that will set the bar for the rest of MusicFest.

Ken Carson

Ken keeps the heat going with his moshpit anthems like "Jennifer's Body" and "Lose it"

Yeat

Day one closes off with Yeat and his alien-like hits such as "Get Busy".


Day Two:

Travis Scott

Day two starts with a bang as Travis takes the stage with an adrenaline-fuelled show.

Drake

No introduction needed. The global superstar makes his MusicFest debut.

Sheck Wes

With hits such as "Mo Bamba" under his belt, Sheck delivers an explosive set.


Day Three:

Yousuke Yukimatsu

Yukimatsu kicks off the final day with an unpredictable set featuring experimental sounds.

Kaytranada

Grammy-winning talent Kaytranada switches things up with a set that mixes funk, soul and house.

Zack Fox

Once a comedic rapper now turned renowned DJ, Zack Fox closes off MusicFest with a set that will bend genres like never before.
